Photo size and framing

Use a portrait JPG or PNG. Each side must be at least 320 px; the longest side must not exceed 4,096 px; file size must be under 9.5 MB. Around 720 × 1,080 px is a good target. One person only: face centred, fully visible and facing forward, with the face wider than 60% of the image width. Use even daylight and avoid coloured lighting or filters. Images larger than 1,080 px on a side are resized automatically.
