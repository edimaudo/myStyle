Choosing a good photo

For the best colour reading, use a clear selfie or head-and-shoulders photo:

  • Stand facing the camera, with just one person in the photo. Keep the whole face centred and make it fill most of the picture.
  • Use soft, even daylight. Skip filters and coloured lighting.
  • Choose a JPG, JPEG or PNG under 9.5 MB. The photo must be at least 320 pixels wide and tall; the longest side can be up to 4,096 pixels.

A photo around 720 × 1,080 pixels works well. Larger photos are resized for you.
