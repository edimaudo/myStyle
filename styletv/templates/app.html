How styleTV works

Explore shows

Start with the shows you love.

Browse daytime soaps, morning shows and talk shows; save favourites to personalize Discover; open WornOnTV to see the actual outfit listings. styleTV’s garment matches are inspiration, not verified on-screen items.

Make it personal

Shape recommendations around your taste.

Use the style quiz or edit your profile to set patterns, colours, boldness and a budget. Recommendations update when your preferences or favourite shows change. Facial colour analysis is optional.

Preview and shop

Try a look before you decide.

Choose a supported garment, upload a photo with your consent, and use YouCam to generate a visual preview. Compare available colour ideas, save looks and follow retailer links for similar items.
